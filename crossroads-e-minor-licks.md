# Crossroads 2026: 5 E minor licks

Eric Clapton's Crossroads Guitar Festival 2026 (Sept 26–27, Moody Center, Austin): Clapton, Buddy Guy, Billy Gibbons, Jimmie Vaughan, Gary Clark Jr., Joe Bonamassa, John Mayer, Derek Trucks, and others.

These five licks are **in the spirit** of that stage. They are not transcriptions. All of them use one box, so you learn one shape and five ways to speak through it.

---

## The only shape you need: E minor pentatonic, 12th fret

```
e|--12--15--|      Root notes (E) are at:
B|--12--15--|        low E 12, D 14, high e 12
G|--12--14--|
D|--12--14--|      Blue note (Bb) to add later:
A|--12--14--|        G string 15, A string 13
E|--12--15--|
```

Play it up and down 5 times before you start. Say "E" out loud each time you hit a root.

---

## Lick 1: "Slowhand Cry" (Clapton)
One bend that sings, then fall home to E.

```
e|-----------12---------------|
B|--15b17--------15--12-------|
G|--------------------14-------|
D|------------------------14~-|
```
- `15b17` = bend the 15th fret up a whole step (to sound like fret 17)
- Hold the bend. Vibrato on the last note. **Slow is the point.**

## Lick 2: "Texas Open E" (Jimmie Vaughan / Gary Clark Jr.)
Open position, down low, with a shuffle feel.

```
e|-----------------------|
B|-----------------------|
G|-----------------------|
D|-------------2--0------|
A|-----0--1--2--------2--|
E|--0--------------------0|
```
- A string fret 1 is the blue note (Bb). Let it rub for a moment, then move on.
- Swing it: long-short, long-short.

## Lick 3: "Rolling Triplets" (Bonamassa)
A 3-note loop you repeat until it becomes a wave.

```
      (repeat 4x)
e|--15p12-------|-----------------|
B|---------15---|--12-------------|
G|--------------|------14---------|
D|--------------|----------14~----|
```
- `p` = pull-off. Pick only the first note on e and the note on B.
- Start at 60 BPM as triplets. Speed comes from repeating it, not from forcing it.

## Lick 4: "Blue Note Wobble" (Buddy Guy / Billy Gibbons)
Creep up to the blue note and back, then land hard.

```
G|--12--14--15--14--12------|
D|-----------------------14~|
```
- G to A to **Bb** to A to G, resolving to E.
- Dig in with the pick. Buddy would hit it louder than you think is polite.

## Lick 5: "Crossroads Walkdown" (the ending lick)
Walk down the box and slide into E through its leading tone (D#). Use it on the last bar.

```
e|--12-------------------------|
B|------15--12-----------------|
G|--------------14--12---------|
D|----------------------13--14~|
```
- D string fret 13 (D#) is the only note outside the box. It pulls you into E.

## The practice pattern: 12-bar blues in E minor

```
| Em  | Em  | Em  | Em  |
| Am  | Am  | Em  | Em  |
| B7  | Am  | Em  | B7  |
```

Where each lick goes:

| Bars | Chord | Play |
|------|-------|------|
| 1–2  | Em | **Lick 1** Slowhand Cry |
| 3–4  | Em | **Lick 2** Texas Open E |
| 5–6  | Am | **Lick 4** Blue Note Wobble |
| 7–8  | Em | **Lick 3** Rolling Triplets |
| 9–12 | B7 → Am → Em | **Lick 5** Walkdown |

Leave space between licks. Half of blues is the rests.

## 7-day plan (15 minutes a day)

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| 1 | Box shape + Lick 1. Tune the bend against fret 17 until it matches. |
| 2 | Lick 2 with a swing feel, then Lick 1 again |
| 3 | Lick 3 at 60 BPM, then 70 |
| 4 | Licks 4 + 5 |
| 5 | Chain all 5 over the 12-bar pattern, slowly |
| 6 | Play along with a Crossroads set. Drop a lick in whenever the band is in E minor. |
| 7 | Change the rhythm of every lick. Now they're yours. |
